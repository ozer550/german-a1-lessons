# German A1 Lessons

**[Open the lessons →](https://ozer550.github.io/german-a1-lessons/)**

27 lessons from the teacher's notes for the A1 German class, plus 13 practice levels. In the sidebar every lesson name has its English meaning in brackets.

- Lessons 1–22: the class's "A1 German Simona LG" Miro board, in the same order as on the board (left to right)
- Lessons 23–24: Akkusativ- and Dativ-Präpositionen, from the 16 Nov class
- Lesson 25: Modalverben (modal verbs), from the 3 Nov class
- Lesson 26: Wechselpräpositionen (two-way prepositions), from the 20 Nov class
- Lesson 27: Kasus-Spickzettel, a one-page case cheat sheet (articles, personal and possessive pronouns, prepositions in Nominativ / Akkusativ / Dativ) with a "Test me" mode and a one-page print button. It's also pinned at the top of the sidebar.

Each lesson has:

- the rules and examples from the board
- a short "Words used here" box at the start that explains any grammar terms (preposition, case, infinitive …) in plain English
- English for every example (translations added for this page are shown in grey italics)
- anything added beyond the class notes in a dashed box (e.g. the möchten table and the sentence-formation section in lesson 25)
- a chapter recap with the key rules and examples
- a quick self-check quiz (tap a question to see the answer)

Student introductions and practice notes are not included.

## Practice

After the lessons, the **Practice** part has 13 levels of multiple-choice questions that build up one step at a time:

1. der, die, das (gender)
2. ein, eine, kein (a / no)
3. Akkusativ-Artikel (accusative articles)
4. Dativ-Artikel (dative articles)
5. Mix: Nom · Akk · Dat (which case?)
6. Personalpronomen (I, you, he…)
7. Pronomen Akk + Dat (me, to me…)
8. Possessivartikel (my, your…)
9. Possessiv Akk + Dat (my, as object)
10. Akkusativ-Präpositionen (für, ohne…)
11. Dativ-Präpositionen (mit, aus…)
12. Wechselpräpositionen (wo or wohin?)
13. Boss-Level (everything mixed, 20 questions)

Each level starts with a short rule card. You get 10 random questions from that level's bank, using sentences and words from the lessons. After each answer you see why it's right, plus the English. Pass with 8 / 10 to get a ✓. In levels 5 and 13 you first pick the case and then the form. At the end you see your mistakes, with a "Retry my mistakes" button. Your progress is saved in your browser.
